Support

Vitek Fitness is the training app I use with my own coaching clients. If something in it does not work, or you have a question about your account, write to me and I will answer personally.

Getting help

Email info@vitekfitness.com. I normally answer within a day or two.

It helps if you say which screen you were on, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. A screenshot is worth a long description.

Getting an account

Accounts are not created inside the app — I set one up for each of my clients and hand over the login. If you train with me and do not have one yet, just ask.

Forgotten password

Tap “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen and follow the link that arrives by email. If the email does not come, check the spam folder, then write to me.

Deleting your account

In the app: the Me tab, then Account, then “Delete my account”. Everything personal is deleted and the login is closed. If you would rather I did it for you, write to me.

Privacy

What the app stores and who can see it is set out on the privacy policy page.
